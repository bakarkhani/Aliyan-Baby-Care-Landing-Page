### Color rules

1. Use deep teal for important headings, calls to action, and navigation emphasis.
2. Use white text on deep-teal buttons.
3. Use mint for selected tabs, subtle highlights, decorative shapes, and hover effects.
4. Use peach sparingly for warmth.
5. Preserve the natural moon-gold, leafy-green, and watercolor tones already present in the mascot artwork.
6. Do not recolor or distort the supplied illustrations simply to force them to match the palette.
7. Avoid neon colors, heavy black sections, oversaturated gradients, excessive shadows, and excessive pastel colors competing at once.
8. Never sacrifice text contrast for aesthetic consistency.
9. Use the CSS variables throughout the application instead of scattering arbitrary hex values across components.
10. Ensure hover, focus, active, disabled, and selected states are visually consistent.

The overall impression should be **warm cream + deep teal + mint**, with soft peach and natural illustration colors as supporting accents.
